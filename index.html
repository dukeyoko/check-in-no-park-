Check-out

  1. Check-out is until 10 am.
  2. Please turn off all the lights.
  3. Leave all the keys on the dining table. Never give keys to the concierge.
  4. Send the host a message when you’re leaving.

Late check-out: unless arranged in advance, $30 per hour applies after 10 am.

House rules

  • Strictly no smoking and no parties or events.
  • Keep noise low, especially between 10 pm and 8 am and when arriving or leaving. Excessive noise can bring building security, a fine under the building rules (up to AU$4,700), eviction and loss of your bond.
  • Visitors must leave by 9 pm. After that, $30 per person per night applies.
  • Guest numbers must match your confirmation or what was agreed in advance. Fees apply for extra guests.
  • When cooking, open the balcony door, not the entrance door. You are responsible for any fire brigade fine caused by the alarm.
  • Follow the building rules in shared areas (pool, gym, sauna, BBQ, lounges, lobby). Children must be supervised on the balcony and in the pool and sauna.
  • Close all windows and doors when you go out.
  • Lost keys cost $150 per set. Lost or unreturned swipe cards or remotes will be charged.
  • Take rubbish to the bins near the lifts on every floor.
  • Leave the apartment as clean as you found it. Dishes, pots, the stove, microwave and oven should be clean and dry and returned to where they belong. Extra cleaning is charged. There’s no need to wash the bed linen or towels.
  • Check the apartment on arrival and tell the host about any damage.

Need anything? Contact the host and we’ll sort it out. Please don’t speak to the concierge. They only work for the building’s residents.
